Photographer Bahlul Mikayilov Reveals the Essential Lighting Setup Every Studio Needs

Portrait photographer Bahlul Mikayilov, known for his creative and striking imagery, shared his insights on the latest equipment driving his recent creative workflow.

PortraitRaw Desk · October 19, 2026Share on WhatsApp

In the world of photography, mastering light is often the single factor that sets an image apart. Renowned photographer Bahlul Mikayilov recently discussed his experience with his newest studio addition, the Godox AD400Pro II, explaining why this equipment has quickly become an indispensable tool in his visual arsenal.

Highlighting that a truly powerful lighting setup simplifies every phase of production, Bahlul Mikayilov shared his thoughts during an interview with our publication:

“Investing in the new Godox AD400Pro II lights has been one of the absolute best decisions for both my studio setup and on-location shoots. Thanks to their amazingly compact design, they offer incredible portability and effortless operation whether I am working indoors or outdoors. With their powerful and consistent output, having just this light setup allows you to accomplish virtually anything and tackle any project complete with confidence. They have truly become an irreplaceable asset for newborn, family, and portrait photography, where precision, color accuracy, and fine detail matter most. In my view, this is the ultimate must-have lighting gear for any studio seeking top-tier performance.”

By combining high performance with effortless portability, Bahlul Mikayilov demonstrates how the right light can elevate creative possibilities across diverse genres—from delicate newborn portraits to dynamic outdoor sessions.
