Thypoch completes its Simera-C cine line with a 100mm T1.9 built for close-ups

The seventh and final Simera-C prime arrives for Sony E and Leica M, priced from $879 and shipping in late October.

PortraitRaw Desk · October 23, 2026Share on WhatsApp

Chinese lens maker Thypoch announced the Simera-C 100mm T1.9 on 23 October in Shenzhen. The company calls it the seventh and final lens in its Simera-C cine series, which now runs 16, 21, 28, 35, 50, 75 and 100mm.

The Sony E version costs $879 (€799) and the Leica M version $959 (€869). Pre-orders are open at B&H and on Thypoch’s own store, with shipping planned for late October.

Thypoch says the lens was designed for character close-ups, with soft, natural bokeh, clear subject detail and controlled chromatic aberration. On a full-frame camera, 100mm is a classic focal length for head-and-shoulders portraits, and the 16-blade iris keeps out-of-focus highlights round.

Optically it uses 11 elements in 8 groups. Focus is manual, with a 210-degree throw, and both the focus and aperture rings carry 0.8 MOD gears for follow-focus systems. It weighs 477 g in Sony E mount (86.4 mm long) and 451 g in Leica M mount (76.6 mm long), takes M67 filters and has a 72 mm front diameter. With Thypoch’s own adapters, the Leica M version can also be used on compatible Sony, Canon, Fujifilm, Lumix and DJI cameras.
